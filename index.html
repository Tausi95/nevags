Company Background

Why NEVAGS Exists

Malawi faces a severe housing crisis — millions of housing units are needed, urbanisation is accelerating, and over 80–90% of all houses in the country are built with fired clay bricks. For decades, these bricks were produced by burning massive quantities of firewood, making Malawi one of the fastest deforesting nations in Africa.

The Malawi Government banned firewood-fired bricks due to the devastating environmental impact — deforestation, soil erosion, flooding, and biodiversity loss. This policy created a critical supply gap: developers, contractors, and communities urgently need a sustainable, affordable, and legally compliant alternative.

NEVAGS Eco Brick & Construction was founded to fill exactly that gap. Operating from Musewu, Mulanje District, we deploy VSK (Vertical Shaft Kiln) technology fuelled by biomass briquettes — producing high-quality bricks without a single stick of firewood. The demand is policy-driven, long-term, and nationwide.

Technology Advantage

Why VSK Technology Matters

The Vertical Shaft Kiln (VSK) is a proven, scalable industrial kiln system that fires bricks at high temperatures using compressed biomass briquettes instead of firewood. It produces consistent, high-quality bricks at lower fuel cost — while remaining 100% compliant with Malawi's environmental regulations.
